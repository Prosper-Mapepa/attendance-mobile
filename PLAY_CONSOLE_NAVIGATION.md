# How to Navigate Play Console - Step by Step

This guide shows you exactly where to find everything in Google Play Console.

## 🎯 Starting Point

You're currently on the **Dashboard**. Here's how to find everything:

## 📍 Finding Store Listing

### Method 1: From Dashboard

1. Look at the **left sidebar** (navigation menu)
2. Find **"Store presence"** section
3. Click on **"Store presence"** to expand it
4. Click **"Main store listing"**

**Path:** `Dashboard → Store presence → Main store listing`

### Method 2: Direct Navigation

1. In the left sidebar, scroll down
2. Look for sections:
   - **Store presence** (expand this)
   - **Policy**
   - **Test and release**
   - **Setup**

3. Under **"Store presence"**, you'll see:
   - Main store listing ← **Click here**
   - Pricing & distribution
   - Content rating (this is different from Policy → Content rating)

## 📍 Finding Content Rating

### Location: Policy Section

1. In the **left sidebar**, find **"Policy"** section
2. Click on **"Policy"** to expand it
3. Click **"App content"**
4. You'll see **"Content rating"** - click on it

**Path:** `Dashboard → Policy → App content → Content rating`

**What you'll see:**
- A button that says "Start questionnaire" or "Edit questionnaire"
- Your current rating status

## 📍 Finding Data Safety

### Location: Policy Section

1. In the **left sidebar**, find **"Policy"** section
2. Click on **"Policy"** to expand it
3. Click **"Data safety"**

**Path:** `Dashboard → Policy → Data safety`

**What you'll see:**
- A form asking about data collection
- Questions about what data your app collects

## 📍 Finding App Access

### Location: Policy Section

1. In the **left sidebar**, find **"Policy"** section
2. Click on **"Policy"** to expand it
3. Click **"App access"**

**Path:** `Dashboard → Policy → App access`

## 📍 Finding Pricing & Distribution

### Location: Store Presence Section

1. In the **left sidebar**, find **"Store presence"** section
2. Click on **"Store presence"** to expand it
3. Click **"Pricing & distribution"**

**Path:** `Dashboard → Store presence → Pricing & distribution`

## 📍 Finding Production Release

### Location: Test and Release Section

1. In the **left sidebar**, find **"Test and release"** section
2. Click on **"Test and release"** to expand it
3. Click **"Production"**

**Path:** `Dashboard → Test and release → Production`

## 🎯 Quick Action Plan

### Step 1: Complete Store Listing (START HERE)

1. **Click "Store presence"** in left sidebar
2. **Click "Main store listing"**
3. Fill in all required fields (marked with red asterisk *)
4. **Save** when done

### Step 2: Complete Content Rating

1. **Click "Policy"** in left sidebar
2. **Click "App content"**
3. **Click "Content rating"**
4. Click **"Start questionnaire"** or **"Edit"**
5. Answer all questions
6. **Submit**

### Step 3: Complete Data Safety

1. **Click "Policy"** in left sidebar
2. **Click "Data safety"**
3. Fill out the form:
   - Does your app collect data? **Yes**
   - What data? **Location, Camera**
   - How is it used? **App functionality**
4. **Save**

### Step 4: Complete Pricing & Distribution

1. **Click "Store presence"** in left sidebar
2. **Click "Pricing & distribution"**
3. Select **"Free"**
4. Select countries (or "All countries")
5. Check compliance boxes
6. **Save**

## 🔍 If You Can't Find Something

### Check the Left Sidebar

The left sidebar should always be visible. If you don't see it:

1. Look for a **hamburger menu** (☰) icon in the top left
2. Click it to expand/collapse the sidebar
3. Make sure you're on the correct app (AttendIQ)

### Use the Search

1. Look for a **search bar** at the top
2. Type what you're looking for:
   - "store listing"
   - "content rating"
   - "data safety"
   - "production"

### Check Publishing Overview

1. Click **"Publishing overview"** in the left sidebar
2. This shows all incomplete tasks
3. Click on any task to go directly to it

## 🆘 Still Can't Find It?

### Common Issues:

1. **Sidebar is collapsed**
   - Look for ☰ menu icon
   - Click to expand

2. **Wrong app selected**
   - Check top of page - should say "AttendIQ"
   - If not, click "All apps" and select AttendIQ

3. **Permissions issue**
   - Make sure you're the app owner or have admin access
   - Check with account owner

4. **App not fully created**
   - Go to: https://play.google.com/console
   - Make sure you see "AttendIQ" in your apps list
   - If not, create the app first
